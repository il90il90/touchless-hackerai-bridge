2 Connect HackerAI

3 Tell HackerAI about it

In HackerAI → Settings → Personalization, paste each text into the field with the same name. Then, in a chat, select the bridge's computer and ask for anything on your phone: an action, an automation, an app to build in Touchless Studio or a mod of an installed app. Edit the texts here if you like; HackerAI takes up to 1500 characters per field.

Open HackerAI

Open it on your phone

The panel is mobile-friendly, so you can run the whole setup from your phone's browser. It needs LAN access on and your PC's LAN IP.

LAN access is off (panel is on this PC only). To reach it from your phone, restart the bridge with the port opened to your network:

BRIDGE_BIND=0.0.0.0 docker compose up -d

Then on your phone's browser open http://<your PC's LAN IP>:8790 — find the IP with ipconfig (Windows) or ip addr. Keep both on the same Wi-Fi. For access from outside your home, use Tailscale rather than forwarding the port.

LAN access is on. On your phone's browser (same Wi-Fi) open http://<your PC's LAN IP>:8790 — find the IP with ipconfig. Log in with your username and password. The panel locks itself after 10 minutes idle.
